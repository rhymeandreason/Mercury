Loudness & duration (technical)

      
Alert and Notify −3 dB (Alert can be raised to 0 dB) · Confirm −6 dB. Confirmations ≤ 900 ms; status changes 600–1,200 ms. Only Battery Low may interrupt another sound. Battery Low at 20/10/5%; Finished repeats once after 60 s untouched.

    

    

      
Hardware notes

      
    
        
  • Buzzer: PUI SMT-0827-S-HT-R, a coil-type transducer (16 Ω) driven by an external square wave. Resonance 2700 ±500 Hz, ≥ 85 dBA at 10 cm (3.6 V0-p, 50% duty). Output falls away from resonance, so the main notes sit in the shaded 2.2–3.2 kHz band.
    • 
        
  • Palette: C‑major pentatonic, mostly C7–G7. Notes from 1.2–2 kHz play about 13 dB quieter than the resonance on this part, and output rises again toward 8 kHz, so square-wave overtones sound harsh. The PUI speaker sim follows the datasheet curve; confirm with a bench test.
    • 
        
  • PWM buzzers can't shape envelopes: step duty cycle (50% → 25% → 10%) to approximate decays, or drop them.
    • 
        
  • Beeps are flat on/off tones, native to PWM: no envelope or pitch slides needed.
    • 
        
  • Pair each sound with a matching LED/haptic pattern for silent mode.
