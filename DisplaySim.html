Actual size assumes browser zoom is 100% (⌘0). The Studio Display has 5120 physical pixels across; the page reads your macOS “Looks like” scaling from the browser so 1× stays true to size at any setting. To check, hold a credit card (85.6 mm wide) against the outline and nudge Fine-tune until they match.

Each lit pixel is drawn as its 0.229 × 0.197 mm emitter inside a 0.249 × 0.217 mm cell, so it is about 15% wider than tall. Artwork drawn with square pixels will look stretched sideways on the real panel; turn on As drawn (Figma) to compare.
